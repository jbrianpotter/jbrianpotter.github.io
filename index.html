PromonTech · Fintech · 2017–2018

Mapping the mortgage, backwards

Problem: A mortgage moves through many hands (borrower, loan officer, outside vendors) and no one could see the whole flow at once.

Role: One of about six designers. I created MUTO (Multi-User Task-Oriented journey), a method that starts at the outcome and works backward, task by task, across every party involved.

Result: Gave the team a shared picture of how work flowed between users and showed where to improve it.
